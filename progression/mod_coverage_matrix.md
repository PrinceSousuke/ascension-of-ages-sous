## Scope

Tracker for meaningful content mod coverage and cross-mod linkage in the live questbook.
This matrix intentionally tracks content systems/clusters, not every utility/library mod in the instance.

Coverage level meanings:

- `none`: no live progression representation
- `incidental`: only touched indirectly or by single isolated mention
- `partial`: bounded starter presence without full arc closure
- `meaningful`: clear chapter identity and progression role
- `full/core`: central to current age progression with explicit cross-mod linkage

Tier meanings:

- `Tier A`: core progression cluster; requires full arc and explicit inbound/outbound links
- `Tier B`: support progression cluster; requires bounded meaningful representation
- `Tier C`: incidental/optional cluster; mention only if it improves flow
