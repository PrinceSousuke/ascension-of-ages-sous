## Current Canon

- Required chapters in the matrix are required; optionality belongs inside chapters.
- Dark Age and Medieval are lean prologues, not giant quest-count buckets.
- Quest density is intentionally back-loaded from Renaissance through Ascension.
- Bosses provide boss-specific proofs only; they do not directly grant major age stages, except the explicit Maledictus FTBQ bridge that still grants `industrial_revolution` as legacy reconciliation debt. IR chapters now exist.
- AStages/FTB Quest capstone rewards own progression proof. KubeJS handles restrictions, recipes, tags, glue, and integration, not stage reads/grants/revokes.
